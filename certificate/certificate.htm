Free Certificate Template for PowerPoint

Download Free Certificate Template for PowerPoint & Google Slides

The Free Certificate Template for PowerPoint is a ready-to-use certificate layout for presentations. The certificate templates are awarded to high achievers and rank holders to appreciate their efforts. For instance, in gaming competitions, exams, and contests, the participants or position holders are awarded a certificate of achievement. Similarly, the employees, teachers, and officials are given certificates for the employee of the month, best teacher, best manager, etc. We have designed this 100% editable certificate design for preparing those certificates. Users can edit the components, color scheme, and placeholder text to generate customized certificates. 

Our Free Certificate Template for PowerPoint features two certificate designs. The first slide has a star badge on the left side. A golden border is added to the slide corresponding to the color of the badge. The text body has three major sections: certificate title, name, and signature space. Presenters can change the certificate title and add the person’s name using the provided space. In the signature area, users can insert an e-signature or use it for in-person signing after printing the slide. The other slide of this free certificate PowerPoint template has similar text segments; however, it varies concerning a red achievement badge on the bottom of the slide. This slide has a delicate border style. 

Professionals can download this free certificate template for PowerPoint and conveniently prepare their certificates for online and in-person presentations. The slides can be printed on customized pages in varying sizes. This free PPT template is ideal for webinars or online ceremonies. Users can also add these slides to their portfolios to show their achievements. Likewise, institutes can add their logo or event details to personalize the certificate. So, download and try this free certificate template for educational or business events. You can edit either slide with all PowerPoint versions, Google Slides, or Keynote.
